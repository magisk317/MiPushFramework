MiPush Manager provides a system-level Xiaomi push service for non-MIUI Android devices.

Core capabilities:
- Centralize Xiaomi push connections through a system-level service.
- Improve notification compatibility across Android devices.
- Inspect registered applications, push events, connection state, and diagnostics.
- Configure notification rules, titles, content, actions, and display behavior.
- Support notification groups, buttons, VoIP styles, rich text, and progress notifications.
- Integrate focus notifications, HyperIsland presentation, and Android live notifications where supported.

MiPush Manager is designed for users who want Xiaomi push compatibility without running a separate push service in every application.
